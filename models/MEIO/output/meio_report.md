## Network scope
- 8 Regional DCs + 15 Customer DCs actively carry demand/inventory in this dataset.
- Replenishment path: Plant -> Plant DC -> Regional DC (-> Customer DC), single-sourced
  via the fastest (min mean lead-time) available lane at each node.
- RDC demand pools its own direct (BULK) volume with the demand of every CDC it primary-sources
  (echelon / installation-stock pooling), so RDC safety stock reflects the full downstream buffer it must support.

## Method
1. Demand mean = mean of 2026 (`horizon == "forward"`) `forecast_qty` per SKU-DC. Demand std = that mean x the
   SKU-DC's 2025 in-sample coefficient of variation of `actual_qty` (capped at 3; median CV where missing). Both
   are taken over raw `demand_forecast.csv` rows, without first aggregating B2B-customer splits to weekly totals.
   RDC std pools as `sqrt(own_std^2 + sum(CDC_std^2))`.
2. Cumulative Plant -> node lead-time mean/variance built by walking the fastest inbound lane at each node, converted to weeks.
3. Target cycle service level per SKU-DC from a newsvendor critical ratio:
   `CR = stockout_cost / (stockout_cost + holding_cost_over_lead_time)`, clipped to [80%, 99.9%], where
   stockout_cost = `margin_usd * stockout_cost_multiplier` and holding_cost_over_lead_time =
   DC-specific weekly holding cost x max(LT_weeks, 1). Then `z = Phi^-1(CR)`, clipped to [0.84, 3.10].
4. Safety stock = `z * sqrt(LT_weeks * sigma_demand^2 + demand_mean^2 * sigma_LT_weeks^2)` (lead time only, no review period).
5. Reorder point = cycle stock (`demand_mean * LT_weeks`) + safety stock; order-up-to = reorder point + 1 week of demand (weekly review).
6. "Current (2025 policy)" = mean of `inventory.csv`'s `safety_stock` per SKU-DC. That value is constant per SKU-DC
   across all 156 recorded weeks (2023-W01..2025-W52), i.e. the baseline policy that ran the history.
