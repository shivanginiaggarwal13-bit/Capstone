Grounding

Synthetic, but not arbitrary

Demand-side parameters are calibrated against a real Food & Beverage Sales dataset. Supply-side parameters are researched CPG benchmarks, not fabricated numbers — flagged clearly so nothing is mistaken for a client fact.

Anchored to real demand data

  • Prices drawn from each SKU's mapped real product group
  • Two elasticities per SKU — base (shelf) and promotional (deal, ~1.75× base) — carried into price response, not shipped as a column
  • Seasonality shape from a mature real year (2020, COVID spike stripped; 2019 excluded as a ramp-up trend)
  • Revenue concentration matches real ~80/20 — realized here: top 20% of SKUs drive 84.7% of revenue

Modelled assumption, not a client fact

  • Unit cost, margin, holding cost — category-differentiated, benchmark-calibrated
  • Logistics cost, lead time, network structure — Coca-Cola-modelled, no real source
  • Production adherence — 93.3% mean, fat left tail for disruption events
  • Forecast accuracy (MAPE) — 6.2% Fast-Moving, 11.6% Intermediate, 21.3% Seasonal
By design, not by omission

What isn't shipped — and has to be derived

Price elasticity is deliberately withheld as a ready-made column, exactly as it would be on a real client extract. It's recovered from orders.csv via a two-way (SKU + week) fixed-effects regression on shelf price and discount depth.
